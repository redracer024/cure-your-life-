# BodySignal Research Protocol

## Purpose

BodySignal explores physical symptoms through biomedical, somatic, psychological, traditional, metaphysical, symbolic, developmental, relationship, and generational lenses.

Research must not stop when conventional medical literature stops discussing emotional or spiritual causation.

The absence of established biomedical proof is a classification issue, not a reason to erase a traditional theory.

## Mandatory research for every condition

Every substantial symptom or condition expansion must investigate all relevant categories below.

### 1. Biomedical causes and mechanisms

Research established causes, mechanisms, differential diagnoses, red flags, testing, treatment context, complications, and safety boundaries.

### 2. Mind / life → body pathways

Search for evidence involving:

- chronic stress
- autonomic state
- sleep

- depression
- trauma-related physiology where relevant
- muscle guarding
- pelvic-floor dysfunction where relevant
- conditioning
- hypervigilance
- avoidance
- pain sensitization
- eating and hydration behavior
- exercise
- substances
- medication behavior
- adherence
- social environment
- occupational factors
- relationship stress
- sexual stress
- healthcare avoidance or delay

Do not invent a direct physiological mechanism when only an indirect behavioral pathway is supported.

### 3. Body → mind consequences

Research effects on:

- mood
- cognition
- identity
- relationships
- sexuality
- fear
- shame
- body trust
- sleep
- motivation
- social participation
- avoidance
- medical behavior

### 4. Metaphysical and spiritual web search

For EVERY condition, deliberately perform searches equivalent to:

- "metaphysical root cause of [condition]"
- "[condition] emotional cause"
- "[condition] emotional root"
- "[condition] spiritual meaning"
- "[condition] spiritual cause"
- "[condition] mind body"
- "[condition] psychosomatic meaning"
- "[condition] symbolic meaning"

Do not assume mainstream search results will surface this material automatically.

### 4A. Biodecoding, biological-decoding, conflict, and emotional-disease systems

Mainstream medical searches frequently do not surface the strongest emotional-causation theories because those theories belong to separate metaphysical, biodecoding, conflict-decoding, and alternative disease-symbolism communities.

For EVERY condition, deliberately search combinations equivalent to:

- "[condition] biodecoding"
- "[condition] biodescodification"
- "[condition] biological decoding"
- "[condition] biological decodification"
- "[condition] emotional conflict"
- "[condition] emotional conflict cause"
- "[condition] emotional origin"
- "[condition] emotional meaning disease"
- "[condition] emotional and spiritual meaning"
- "[condition] subconscious conflict"
- "[condition] spiritual nutrition"
- "[condition] Recall Healing"
- "[condition] Total Biology"
- "[condition] German New Medicine"
- "[condition] Hamer conflict"

Also search important subtypes separately when the condition contains medically distinct disorders.

Example for testicular problems:

- "testicular torsion biodecoding"
- "varicocele emotional conflict"
- "epididymitis emotional meaning"
- "azoospermia biodecoding"
- "cryptorchidism emotional cause"
- "testicular cyst emotional meaning"
- "testicular cancer biodecoding"

The purpose of these searches is NOT to endorse the systems.

The purpose is to accurately discover what the systems actually propose so BodySignal does not claim to provide a metaphysical root map while ignoring the schools most explicitly devoted to emotional root-cause theories.

When these sources make strong causal claims, report those claims faithfully as claims of that system.

Example:

"Some biodecoding sources propose a separation or loss conflict as a root pattern for testicular disorders."

Do NOT silently rewrite that into:

"Separation is merely a metaphor for testicular disease."

But also do NOT rewrite it into:

"Separation causes testicular cancer."

BodySignal must preserve the intellectual category.

Use:

"Within this biodecoding framework..."

"Some emotional-disease-symbolism sources propose..."

"This system interprets..."

"This source attributes..."

Then separately state whether modern biomedical evidence supports the proposed disease mechanism.

These systems often disagree with one another. Do not invent consensus.

A condition-specific claim found on one website is not automatically:

- universal metaphysical teaching
- ancient wisdom
- Traditional Chinese Medicine
- Ayurveda
- Jungian psychology
- a teaching of Louise Hay, Dahlke, Bourbeau, Martel, Myss, Reich, Lowen, or another named author

Source families must remain distinct.

Especially scrutinize claims involving:

- cancer
- congenital abnormalities
- pregnancy
- fetal development
- abuse
- sexual assault
- death or bereavement
- childhood trauma
- inherited or ancestral conflict
- infectious disease
- genetic disease

Such claims may be presented as part of the metaphysical system when genuinely sourced, but must not be converted into established medical causation or used to blame the person who became ill.

## Source-fidelity rule

Named-source attribution requires verification.

There are three acceptable levels:

### Direct named-author attribution

Use only when the author can be verified as making the condition-specific claim.

### Framework-level attribution

Use when the author's broader system is verified but the exact disease-specific statement is not.

Example:

"Within Dahlke-style disease symbolism..."

### BodySignal synthesis

Use when BodySignal is applying a broader symbolic framework.

Example:

"A Jungian-style lens might explore..."

Never turn AI-generated synthesis into a fake quotation or author doctrine.

## Causation rule

BodySignal may faithfully state that a metaphysical, energetic, traditional, or historical system proposes emotional or spiritual causation.

Do not weaken a source into "mere metaphor" when the source itself explicitly claims causation.

At the same time:

- do not present the traditional claim as established biomedical fact
- do not invent a biochemical mechanism to make the traditional theory sound scientific
- do not tell users they emotionally caused their disease as established fact
- do not allow symbolic reflection to delay urgent medical evaluation

Use language such as:

"Within this tradition, unresolved fear is proposed as a contributor to Kidney-system imbalance."

or:

"Disease-symbolism authors have interpreted stones as the physical expression of emotional hardening."

Then separately state the modern evidence status.

## Evidence classification

Use:

1. Established / Well-Supported
2. Plausible / Indirect
3. Uncertain / Emerging
4. Traditional / Metaphysical
5. Not Supported as a Biomedical Cause

A claim may be Traditional / Metaphysical and still deserve substantial exploration.

## Two-axis audit

Every condition should receive two separate judgments.

### Axis A — Biomedical mind→body evidence

A — strong / meaningful

B — plausible / indirect

C — mainly affects symptoms, course, behavior, or experience

D — no credible psychological disease-origin pathway established

### Axis B — Traditional / metaphysical richness

1 — very rich
2 — moderate
3 — limited but useful
4 — forcing it would be nonsense

Do not use low Axis A as a reason to erase high Axis B material.

## Research-depth rule

A research report must contain actual researched material.

Do not return instructions such as:

- "Explore fear"
- "Discuss Root chakra"
- "Consider emotional stagnation"
- "Look into family patterns"

Instead explain:

- what the source or tradition proposes
- why the association exists within that system
- what evidence exists or does not exist
- what somatic or behavioral parallel may exist
- why the systems are not automatically equivalent
- what reflection questions follow
- what safety boundary applies

## Web-search responsibility

The researcher is responsible for finding this material.

The user should not have to search the web manually for every symptom because the research agent stopped after mainstream medical sources.

If useful spiritual or metaphysical material can be found through ordinary public web searches, search for it.

## Final BodySignal principle

Explore the whole pattern.

Do not reduce physical illness to psychology.

Do not reduce human experience to laboratory findings.

Do not erase traditional theories because modern medicine does not accept their mechanisms.

Do not convert traditional theories into proven biomedical facts.

Show the maps clearly enough that the user can understand which map they are reading.
